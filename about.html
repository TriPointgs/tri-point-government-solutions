About Tri-Point

Founded on June 20, 2026, Tri-Point Government Solutions LLC is a Florida small business that helps government agencies, schools and colleges, businesses, nonprofits, and houses of worship source technology, equipment, and supplies. Our backgrounds in IT and equipment maintenance shape how we approach every purchase. We ask how your team will use what it buys and what it will take to keep it working after delivery.

Meet our founders

Our Mission

We help customers keep their operations running. We learn what machines and systems you use, confirm which parts and supplies fit, and keep track of the reorders, renewals, and warranty dates we’ve agreed to manage. We reach out before those dates so your team has time to plan. If a product needs support, we help coordinate with the manufacturer or service provider and keep you updated. You shouldn’t have to chase us for an answer.

Our Vision

We want to be the supplier customers call first because we already understand what they use, what they need next, and how to help when something goes wrong.

Our Goals

  • Make purchasing easier with clear quotes, confirmed compatibility, and realistic lead times.
  • Help customers avoid preventable downtime by planning ahead for supplies, replacement parts, and renewals.
  • Earn repeat business by giving customers a point of contact who takes responsibility for following up after the sale.

Our Founders

Adriel Lorenzo

Co-Founder | CEO

As one of the founders of Tri-Point Government Solutions and its CEO, I lead our business development, government contracting efforts, and vendor relationships. I stay personally involved in understanding what buyers need and bringing together the products, people, and resources to meet those requirements. Building a small business has made me appreciate how much trust comes from being accessible, communicating clearly, and doing what you say you will do.

My background spans hospitality, industrial maintenance, process serving, and entrepreneurship. Working with complex equipment in hotel laundry and car-wash operations taught me to troubleshoot carefully, adapt when the first approach doesn’t work, and understand how a purchasing decision affects the people relying on that equipment every day. My earlier work in process serving reinforced the importance of accurate documentation, deadlines, and following requirements closely. Running businesses has brought those lessons together, adding experience in customer service, sourcing, fulfillment, and the daily responsibility of making things work.

Those experiences shape how I lead Tri-Point. I ask questions, research the details, and stay involved when a request becomes difficult. I want buyers to understand their options, know what to expect, and feel comfortable reaching out when something needs attention. As we grow, my priority is to build a company people can rely on throughout the purchasing process and after the order arrives.

Bobby Henry

Co-Founder | CFO

As one of the founders of Tri-Point Government Solutions and its Chief Financial Officer, I oversee our finances and work directly with the government buyers we serve. One of the things I value most about being part of a small business is that I can stay involved from the first conversation through the final quote. I take time to understand what a buyer needs, then work with our vendors to source products that fit the agency’s technical requirements and budget.

My background is in IT, where years of working with a wide range of technologies taught me to ask better questions and look at a problem from more than one angle. That experience shapes how I approach procurement. When a product is hard to find, I’ll dig into the specifications, talk through the options with vendors, and look for another way to meet the original need.

Customers and colleagues know me for that tenacity. I enjoy the work of untangling a difficult request, and I want every buyer who works with me to understand their options and feel confident about the next step.
