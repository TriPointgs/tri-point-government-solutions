## Archive handling and corrections

The supplied HuddleCamHD ZIP is truncated and has no readable central directory. It was not rewritten. Sequential local-file headers permit seeking past complete entries without loading videos. Only complete, bounded photo/PDF records were extracted. This made exact imagery available for the legacy MiniTrack, Pro/Pro IP, Webcam104, HuddlePair, HuddleShare and other illustrated legacy cameras. No truncated video was used.

The Studio SE SKU text in the 2025 catalog repeats Studio 4K identifiers. This catalog uses the actual Studio SE model folders and data sheet, not that typo. Studio Pro is labeled legacy, consistent with the supplied catalog's end-of-life annotation. Link 4K does not imply Dante transports 4K60: the description distinguishes supported 4K outputs from Dante AV-H up to 1080p60.

## Explicit limits and missing pieces

- Legacy PT Joy G3, WirelessCable (`PT-WC-G1`, `PT-WC-PP-G1`), HC Joy G3 and certain older HuddleCam models appear as documentation-only or incomplete records. They do not receive misleading cards with an unrelated product photo. The supplied images available to this review do not establish full image coverage for those models.
- HuddleCam `HC-PSB` and `HC-PSW` are named in an old catalog, but exact replacement images/model compatibility were not established; they remain quote-by-model inquiries rather than invented photographed products.
- Standalone utilities such as CMP, OBS plug-ins/dockable controllers, device manager, and firmware tools are linked through official downloads instead of being presented as separately stocked commercial products. Vendor HTML/software was not executed.
- Flex/custom bundle combinations are a configuration service, not a single fixed SKU. The public page may link the official bundle resource and invite a configuration inquiry; it should not claim a discount or default bill of materials.
- Per-variant color/lens availability, region, replacement lifecycle, current software plans and installed-firmware support require quote-time verification. Supplied photography does not prove resale eligibility or present stock.
