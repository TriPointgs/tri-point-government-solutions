# Tri-Point — First Contractor Acquisition Sprint

## The target
Aim for qualified, contactable contractors—not an inflated signup count. Start in Lake, Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Volusia, Marion and Sumter counties. Prioritize cleaning, landscaping, printing/mailing and facility trades where Tri-Point is actively evaluating real solicitations. Expand only after useful coverage and reliable follow-through.

## Initial channels and attribution
Share the contractor landing page publicly; use the join link when asking for an application. Examples:
- https://tripointgs.com/contractors/join?utm_source=facebook&utm_campaign=central-florida-launch
- https://tripointgs.com/contractors/join?utm_source=partner&utm_campaign=trade-association-launch
- https://tripointgs.com/contractors/join?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=central-florida-introductions
The form stores only allowed source names and a sanitized campaign label. Do not put contact names or email addresses in tracking parameters.

## Useful content topics
Record a short explanation of the difference between joining a network and qualifying for a solicitation; why a cleaner should price actual staffing hours; why a print shop must separate postage from production; why a landscaping bid needs route costs; and how to identify a mandatory site visit. Link each post to the relevant guide, not to invented contract listings. Do not call a closed opportunity open or use hypothetical award values as facts.

## Weekly operating review — manual until scheduled
Count applications by source, trade and county; identify missing fields; contact relevant applicants; confirm capacity; review opt-in and opt-out handling; measure meaningful responses to actual opportunities. Track: application -> relevant profile -> contacted -> scope/quote received -> bid participation -> actual award/performance. The current directory supports intake, fit review and export; later-stage CRM statuses need a separate implementation.

## Before spending or scaling
Confirm end-to-end conversion, reliable response ownership, marketing permissions, sender deliverability, opportunity freshness and performance evidence. Do not buy backlinks, mass-create thin city pages, send bulk unsolicited messages, or purchase ads without an approved budget and a conversion baseline.
